What this is not

BGET is a community, not a funnel. We are deliberately not:

Not a lead-gen list Not a Discord mass-invite Not a networking club Not pay-to-join Not a follower count

Membership is free. What it costs is honesty, effort and the willingness to be judged on character — not on a job title.

Questions people actually ask

Before you apply

Straight answers to what we're asked most. If yours isn't here, the application covers it — or write to nikhilnagpure1111@gmail.com.

Do I need credentials, a degree, or a famous portfolio?

No. There is no credential threshold. Proof beats titles — something you built, fixed, run or improved, with links if you have them.

What if I'm early in my career — or not a "techie" at all?

BGET is deliberately multidisciplinary: scientists, engineers, makers, founders, designers, medics, teachers, tradespeople and thinkers. The essays matter more than the job title.

Is there a fee to join?

No. Joining costs time and effort, not money. BGET is not pay-to-join, and never will be.

How long until I hear back?

Usually within 14 days, and you will hear either way. A kind, honest no is part of respecting your time.

Which countries are eligible?

Every country. Eligibility has no geography — timezones and regions are deliberately balanced so the community never becomes one place talking to itself.

What happens after I apply?

You receive an application ID, your answers are read in full, and if there is a fit you are invited to a real conversation with a member before anything is confirmed.

If I'm declined, can I apply again?

Yes, when something has genuinely changed — not the same application. The friction is deliberate: it keeps intent high and the community honest.

What if my work doesn't look like a "project"?

Building a team, a family, a farm, a community or a curriculum counts. We ask what changed because you existed — that is the proof.
