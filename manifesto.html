Vision & Philosophy

BGET — Build. Grow. Evolve. Together.

The Vision

BGET is envisioned as a global, multidisciplinary community and eventually an ecosystem of exceptional human beings who come together to build, grow, evolve, learn, help one another, and create meaningful positive impact on the world.

At its heart, BGET is about bringing together people with different forms of intelligence, knowledge, skills, experiences, perspectives, professions, cultures, and dreams — and creating an environment where those differences become a source of collective intelligence and collective action.

The ultimate ambition is not simply to create another online community, networking group, developer server, startup, or professional organization.

The long-term vision is to create a place where human capability compounds.

A place where exceptional minds can meet exceptional hearts, learn from one another, build together, solve difficult problems, and use their combined capabilities to contribute toward a more advanced, peaceful, harmonious, sustainable, and capable human civilization.

01

What BGET Stands For

B — Build

Build things that matter.

Build software, companies, research, technologies, communities, organizations, solutions, ideas, infrastructure, knowledge, relationships, and better versions of ourselves.

BGET should encourage people to move from:

Idea → Action → Experiment → Solution → Real-world impact.

The community should not be centered around endlessly talking about what should be done.

It should encourage people to actually do it.

If something is broken, try to improve it.

If a problem exists, investigate it.

If an opportunity exists, explore it.

If an idea is worth pursuing, build it.

G — Grow

Growth happens individually and collectively.

Members should help one another grow:

  • intellectually
  • technically
  • professionally
  • physically
  • mentally
  • emotionally
  • socially
  • spiritually
  • financially
  • creatively
  • strategically
  • ethically

Growth should not be viewed purely through career advancement or money.

A person should ideally become more capable without becoming less human.

BGET should be a place where people raise each other's standards while remaining supportive and compassionate.

E — Evolve

BGET is about continuously becoming better.

Members should be willing to:

  • question themselves
  • challenge assumptions
  • learn from mistakes
  • accept criticism
  • change their minds when evidence demands it
  • learn outside their fields
  • understand perspectives different from their own
  • develop new capabilities
  • improve their character
  • become wiser alongside becoming more capable

Evolution applies to individuals, teams, projects, organizations, and eventually civilization itself.

T — Together

The word "together" is fundamental.

BGET is built on the belief that collective intelligence can accomplish things that isolated individuals cannot.

A mathematician has knowledge that an engineer may not.

A doctor sees problems that a software engineer may never encounter.

A cybersecurity researcher sees threats that an entrepreneur may overlook.

A civil engineer understands physical infrastructure.

A scientist understands phenomena at a deeper level.

A strategist understands systems.

A philosopher challenges assumptions.

An entrepreneur understands execution and markets.

A designer understands human interaction.

A community member understands lived experience.

When these perspectives come together, a problem can be examined from multiple dimensions rather than through a single professional lens.

That multidisciplinary collaboration is one of the central mechanisms of BGET.

03

Intelligence Is Not Limited to IQ

BGET wants extremely capable people, including some of the smartest minds available across different fields.

However, intelligence should not be reduced to a numerical IQ score.

BGET should value:

  • analytical intelligence
  • technical intelligence
  • mathematical intelligence
  • scientific reasoning
  • creativity
  • practical intelligence
  • emotional intelligence
  • social intelligence
  • strategic thinking
  • intuition
  • wisdom
  • leadership
  • resilience
  • craftsmanship
  • domain expertise
  • curiosity
  • ability to learn

Someone may have extraordinary potential even if they have not yet had the opportunity to demonstrate it.

Therefore:

Character + potential + capability + trajectory matter more than credentials alone.

04

The Ideal BGET Person

The ideal BGET member is not necessarily perfect.

They are someone who genuinely wants to become better.

They are:

Genuine

They don't constantly perform a fake version of themselves to impress others.

Humble

They can be extremely capable without believing they are superior to everyone else.

They can say:

"I don't know."

And:

"You're better at this than me. Teach me."

Ambitious

They have dreams.

They want to accomplish something meaningful with their life.

They don't want to simply drift through existence.

Hardworking

They don't merely talk about their ambitions.

They act.

Proactive

They see something that can be improved and take initiative.

Solution-oriented

Their instinct is:

"Okay, this is a problem. What can we do about it?"

rather than simply complaining.

Helpful

They want to help people even when there is nothing immediately in it for them.

Compassionate

They genuinely care about other people.

They try to understand before judging.

God-conscious / spiritually grounded

They care about morality and believe their actions matter even when nobody is watching.

Ethical

They don't believe that achieving a goal justifies harming people.

They understand that capability comes with responsibility.

Positive

Not fake positivity or denial of problems.

Rather, the belief that problems are worth attempting to solve.

Resilient

They possess strong willpower.

When something genuinely matters to them, they are willing to:

  • learn
  • fail
  • adapt
  • try again
  • persist
  • find another way

Curious

They want to understand how things work.

Multidisciplinary

They are willing to learn outside their specialization.

Collaborative

They don't see another person's intelligence as a threat.

They see it as an opportunity to learn.

Playful

They can work incredibly hard while still laughing, joking, playing games, exploring hobbies and enjoying life.

Human

They have dreams outside of work.

They want friendships, families, adventures, experiences, creativity, spirituality, peace, happiness and meaningful lives.

BGET should never become a place where people believe they must grind 18 hours a day to prove their worth.

05

Character Before Capability

One of the most important principles of BGET is:

Exceptional capability without character is not enough.

A person can be brilliant and still be wrong for BGET.

Someone can have:

  • an extraordinary résumé
  • enormous technical ability
  • prestigious credentials
  • high intelligence
  • impressive achievements

and still lack the qualities necessary for a healthy community.

BGET therefore prioritizes:

Character + integrity + kindness + humility + responsibility

alongside:

Intelligence + competence + ambition + execution.

The goal is not to create the most powerful people possible.

It is to create highly capable people who can be trusted with their capabilities.

06

BGET's Culture

The desired atmosphere is:

Deep but playful.

Ambitious but humble.

Hardworking but relaxed.

Spiritual but not judgmental.

Intelligent but not arrogant.

Competitive but supportive.

Serious about purpose but not serious about every little thing.

Someone should be able to discuss:

  • consciousness
  • physics
  • artificial intelligence
  • God
  • civilization
  • philosophy
  • geopolitics
  • cybersecurity
  • mathematics
  • climate
  • medicine

and then five minutes later:

  • play chess
  • share memes
  • joke around
  • talk about music
  • discuss travel
  • talk about their hobbies
  • simply hang out

BGET should be a human community, not a corporate conference room.

07

BGET Should Help People Believe in Good People Again

A deeper purpose of the community is to create an environment where people experience genuine kindness.

Members should encounter people who:

  • help without expecting something in return
  • share knowledge
  • encourage others
  • introduce people to opportunities
  • celebrate other people's success
  • respectfully disagree
  • support people when they're struggling
  • give others credit
  • admit mistakes
  • genuinely want others to succeed

Instead of simply telling people:

"There are still good people in the world."

BGET should allow them to experience it.

08

The Problem-Solver Philosophy

BGET should develop people who don't simply identify problems.

They attempt to solve them.

The philosophy is:

Don't just point at the problem. Be part of the solution.

This does not mean blindly rushing into action.

Critical thinking remains essential.

Members should:

  1. Understand the problem.
  2. Investigate its causes.
  3. Identify constraints.
  4. Gather relevant expertise.
  5. Consider multiple perspectives.
  6. Develop possible solutions.
  7. Examine risks and unintended consequences.
  8. Build or test something.
  9. Measure whether it actually works.
  10. Learn from failure.
  11. Improve the solution.
  12. Deploy it responsibly.

This is knowledge converted into action.

09

Collective Intelligence

One of the central beliefs behind BGET is that:

No single person needs to know everything when extraordinary people can learn from and complement one another.

Consider a problem such as providing clean water to a remote community.

It may require:

  • civil engineering
  • hydrology
  • chemistry
  • biology
  • electrical engineering
  • software
  • data science
  • economics
  • logistics
  • government knowledge
  • psychology
  • local community knowledge
  • entrepreneurship
  • environmental science

No single profession owns the entire solution.

BGET's purpose is to bring those perspectives together.

The community becomes a network in which:

knowledge connects to knowledge,

people connect to people,

ideas connect to ideas,

and eventually:

capability connects to capability.

10

Becoming a Problem Solver

A personal aspiration within this philosophy is to become a highly capable generalist.

The goal is not necessarily to literally become the world's greatest expert at every individual discipline.

Rather, the goal is to develop the ability to:

  • understand complex problems
  • learn rapidly
  • connect different fields
  • identify important questions
  • reason about systems
  • understand enough of multiple disciplines to communicate with specialists
  • find the right experts
  • combine expertise
  • evaluate solutions
  • turn knowledge into action

Deep expertise remains valuable.

But real-world problems often cross disciplinary boundaries.

Therefore BGET encourages:

Depth where it matters + breadth where it creates connections.

Another team could work on:

  • public transportation

13

Public-Good Work

Not everything BGET builds needs to become a commercial business.

Some projects could deliberately be created for public benefit.

Examples include:

  • free software
  • open-source tools
  • free educational platforms
  • government technology
  • environmental monitoring
  • humanitarian systems
  • research
  • accessibility tools
  • nonprofit infrastructure
  • public datasets
  • community resources

Where appropriate, BGET could provide solutions to:

  • governments
  • NGOs
  • schools
  • communities
  • researchers
  • humanitarian organizations
  • people who cannot otherwise afford them

The guiding question becomes:

"Can this capability create meaningful positive impact?"

rather than:

"How much money can we extract from it?"

14

Entrepreneurship Still Has a Place

BGET is not anti-business.

Entrepreneurship can be one of the mechanisms through which useful ideas become sustainable and scalable.

Members can:

  • create startups
  • build products
  • launch companies
  • raise capital
  • commercialize technologies
  • create jobs
  • develop sustainable businesses

Some BGET projects may become companies.

Some may remain open source.

Some may become nonprofits.

Some may become research projects.

Some may simply help a small community.

Impact determines the path.

15

The Civilization-Level Vision

The ultimate aspiration goes beyond individual projects.

BGET aims to contribute, however modestly, toward a future in which humanity becomes:

  • more scientifically capable
  • more technologically advanced
  • more educated
  • more resilient
  • more cooperative
  • more peaceful
  • more compassionate
  • more environmentally responsible
  • more capable of solving complex problems

The ambition is to help humanity progress toward a more advanced civilization without sacrificing the things that make civilization worth having.

That means:

More technology — without destroying nature.

More power — with more responsibility.

More intelligence — with more wisdom.

More ambition — with more humility.

More progress — with more compassion.

More capability — with more ethical responsibility.

16

Nature Is Non-Negotiable

Human advancement should not come at the cost of destroying the natural systems that sustain life.

BGET therefore wants to care about:

  • nature
  • biodiversity
  • ecosystems
  • climate
  • sustainable resource use
  • environmental health
  • long-term planetary sustainability

The goal is not:

"Advance humanity at any cost."

It is:

"Advance humanity while sustaining the world that humanity depends upon."

A better civilization should ultimately be better for both people and the planet.

17

Peace and Harmony

The vision is not technological dominance.

BGET should promote:

  • cooperation
  • peaceful problem-solving
  • mutual respect
  • understanding
  • dialogue
  • empathy
  • ethical responsibility

Different people will have different:

  • cultures
  • beliefs
  • religions
  • political views
  • professions
  • philosophies
  • experiences

BGET should be capable of bringing them together around shared positive objectives without requiring everyone to become identical.

The objective is unity without uniformity.

18

Quality Over Quantity

BGET should not optimize for member count.

The objective is not:

100,000 random Discord members.

The objective is:

A network of genuinely exceptional human beings who trust one another and create extraordinary things together.

The first 5–10 people matter enormously.

The first 50 matter enormously.

Culture should be protected while the network grows.

A small group of exceptional, trustworthy people can be more valuable than thousands of inactive or incompatible members.

19

How People Should Be Evaluated

Credentials alone should not determine membership.

A person should ideally be evaluated through:

Character

Are they genuine?

Intent

Do they actually care about people and positive impact?

Capability

What can they do?

Potential

What could they become?

Initiative

Do they take action?

Curiosity

Do they want to learn?

Humility

Can they admit when they're wrong?

Collaboration

Do they make others better?

Integrity

Can they be trusted?

Trajectory

Are they continuously improving?

Life balance

Can they work hard while still living a healthy, joyful life?

The goal is to identify good humans with exceptional potential or capability.

20

BGET Should Be a Magnet, Not a Marketplace

Rather than desperately collecting members, BGET should become the kind of place exceptional people naturally want to join.

The goal is for someone to discover BGET and think:

"These are my people."

That means the community's culture must be stronger than its marketing.

People should join because they see:

  • interesting minds
  • meaningful projects
  • genuine friendships
  • intellectual curiosity
  • kindness
  • ambition
  • humor
  • action
  • positive impact
21

Growth Through Trust

The ideal growth mechanism is not random mass recruitment.

It is:

Good people → good experiences → trust → relationships → introductions → more good people.

One exceptional member knows another exceptional person.

That person knows someone else.

Eventually BGET becomes a network of trusted introductions.

This allows quality to compound.

22

BGET Should Never Become an Ego Club

Exceptional people must not gather merely to prove they are exceptional.

The purpose of intelligence is not status.

The purpose of capability is not superiority.

The purpose of knowledge is not to dominate others.

The purpose of expertise is to create, understand, help and solve.

The community should encourage people to become extraordinary while remaining grounded.

Ambition + Humility

The Ultimate Goal

The ultimate goal of BGET is not simply to create successful individuals.

It is not simply to create successful companies.

It is not simply to create a large Discord server.

It is not simply to produce impressive technology.

The ultimate goal is:

To bring capable, curious, genuine and compassionate human beings together so that their combined knowledge, character, creativity and determination can be used to build a better future.

A future where humanity can become:

more capable,

more knowledgeable,

more technologically advanced,

more peaceful,

more compassionate,

more sustainable,

BGET in One Paragraph

BGET — Build. Grow. Evolve. Together — is a global multidisciplinary community for genuine, ambitious, curious, hardworking and compassionate people who want to become more capable while remaining deeply human. It seeks to bring together exceptional minds and people with exceptional potential across science, medicine, engineering, technology, cybersecurity, mathematics, research, business, entrepreneurship, strategy, philosophy, management, arts and countless other disciplines. By combining different forms of intelligence and experience, BGET aims to create collective intelligence that can move beyond discussion into real action: building projects, conducting research, creating companies and nonprofits, developing open-source and free public-good solutions, helping communities, protecting nature and solving meaningful problems. At its deepest level, BGET seeks to help people build better lives while contributing toward a more advanced, peaceful, harmonious and sustainable human civilization — advancing human capability without sacrificing human dignity, compassion, ethics, or the natural world.
